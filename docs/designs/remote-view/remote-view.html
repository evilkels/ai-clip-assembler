Remote View — the app on an iPhone over the Editor's tailnet

Mobile-first screens in the desktop's own tokens (neutral scale, rose accent, mono labels), following the OS light/dark setting. Connection state is on every screen; tapping it is how the Editor disconnects. Pairing is QR plus approval on the Mac; uploads say when the Mac has verified a file; Phone Exports say "Shared", never "Saved to Photos". Internals follow docs/specs/2026-10-10-remote-view-architecture.md.
